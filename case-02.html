02Product strategy

Defining what to build for a strategic partner

CompanyIntegral Ad Science
RoleSolutions Engineer
DeliverableProduct assessment & solution design
Challenge

A strategic platform partner had a need that our existing products could not fully address. We needed to define the requirements and determine what product capabilities to build.

What I did

Led discovery with the partner, translated its needs into product requirements, assessed current capabilities and gaps, and worked with Product and Engineering to compare alternative approaches.

Outcome

Defined product direction and the capabilities needed to address the partner’s requirements.

Context

A strategic platform partner was exploring new product capabilities as part of a competitive opportunity. Their requirements extended beyond our existing product offerings, so we needed to define what new capabilities to build and how they would fit alongside our current products.

Approach

I started by understanding the partner's objectives and expected functionality, then translated them into product requirements. Using our existing capabilities as a baseline, I defined what needed to be built and worked with Product and Engineering to scope and compare approaches.

Solution

Define the product opportunity

I led discovery conversations with the partner to understand their objectives, the users the capability would serve, and how success would be measured. I translated these conversations into product requirements and prioritized them into must-have and nice-to-have capabilities.

Assess current product capabilities

I mapped each requirement against our existing product capabilities to identify what was already supported, what could be extended, and what would need to be built. This defined the scope of new development and gave us a baseline for comparing product approaches.

Evaluate the build path

Working with Product and Engineering, I developed alternative approaches for delivering the new capabilities and evaluated them against partner fit, implementation complexity, and development effort.

Impact

01Product requirements

Translated the partner's need into clear, prioritized product requirements.

02Build scope

Identified which capabilities to extend and which to build new.

03Product direction

Recommended an approach that met the partner's needs and fit within the existing product portfolio.
