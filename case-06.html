06Competitive strategy

Winning a head-to-head evaluation against an incumbent

CompanyIntegral Ad Science
RoleSolutions Engineer
DeliverableTest design & performance optimization
Challenge

A high-stakes head-to-head evaluation to displace an incumbent competitor.

What I did

Designed the testing methodology, tracked performance daily in a central tracker and presented mid-campaign recommendations.

Outcome

Demonstrated product and partnership value, resulting in a competitive win.

Context

Given their revenue potential, head-to-head product evaluations receive heightened scrutiny from both customers and internal leadership. Success requires more than strong product performance. It also requires a well-designed testing methodology, ongoing performance monitoring, and a strong customer partnership.

Approach

Using the advertiser’s success criteria, I designed the testing methodology and recommended the products, configurations, and implementation approach best suited to achieving their objectives.

Solution

Centralized performance tracking

I created a centralized campaign performance tracker to provide Sales, Solutions Engineering, and senior leadership with visibility into the evaluation. Updated daily, the tracker consolidated performance metrics, benchmark comparisons, key findings, and configuration changes, making it easier for stakeholders to understand campaign progress without having to pull individual reports.

Performance analysis and optimization

I translated campaign data into actionable recommendations, identifying the drivers of performance fluctuations and opportunities for improvement. I developed and presented mid-campaign findings to the customer, explaining performance trends, recommending configuration changes, and demonstrating how our products could better support their objectives.

Impact

01New business win

Demonstrated product and partnership value against an incumbent competitor leading to a win.

02Customer trust

Strengthened the relationship by acting as a hands-on partner throughout the evaluation.

03Leadership visibility

Centralized performance insights for leadership to inform commercial decisions.
