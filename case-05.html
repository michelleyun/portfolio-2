05Consumer insights

Turning consumer insights into actionable marketing strategies

CompanyNexxen
RoleAnalyst, Advanced Insights & Data
DeliverableClient-facing research presentations
Challenge

Sales needed research-backed collateral to open prospect conversations, while clients needed audience insights to inform media strategy.

What I did

Analyzed consumer interests, behaviors, demographics, and trends, then translated the findings into client presentations to inform media buying strategies.

Outcome

Research that helped Sales open conversations and helped brand and agency teams plan campaigns.

Context

At Nexxen, my research supported both new business conversations and campaign planning. Sales used tailored research presentations to start conversations with prospects about their customers and market. For brand and agency teams planning a media buy, the research provided a basis for deciding which audiences to prioritize and why.

Approach

I worked with Sales to understand what each client wanted to learn, then used a proprietary consumer intelligence platform and indexed scores to compare segments, find what made audiences distinct, and spot timely trends.

Solution

Audience research and media recommendations

I translated research findings into client-facing presentations that combined data visualizations, consumer insights, and actionable recommendations. These included audience profiles, competitive comparisons, and trend analysis, tailored to the client’s business objectives.

Impact

01Sales enablement

Strategic materials for opening conversations with prospective clients.

02Audience strategy

Clear recommendations on who to target and why.

03Market intelligence

Timely insights into emerging trends and major events.
