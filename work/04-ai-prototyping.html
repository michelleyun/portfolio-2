04AI prototyping

Bringing a product experience to life with AI

CompanyIntegral Ad Science
RoleSolutions Engineer
DeliverableInteractive product demo
Challenge

A partner wanted to explore the advertiser reporting experience, but there was no interactive demo and real advertiser data could not be shared.

What I did

Used generative AI to turn existing product designs into a browser-based prototype with synthetic data, building and refining it without dedicated engineering support.

Outcome

The partner could explore the advertiser reporting experience firsthand, without needing platform access or real data.

Context

A platform partner wanted to understand how advertisers would experience the reporting platform and asked for screenshots or a video walkthrough. Static screenshots would not truly show how the product worked, and a live demo using real advertiser data was not an option.

Approach

I wanted to give the partner a useful way to evaluate the product, rather than just send static screens. My goal was to make the experience as realistic and explorable as possible, using data that reflected the partner’s environment, while protecting real data.

Solution

Interactive product demo

A shareable, interactive product demo that allowed the partner to explore the reporting experience without requiring platform access or exposing real advertiser data. The prototype recreated key elements of the advertiser experience: reporting dashboards, interactive filters and dropdown menus, metric definitions and tooltips, and reporting tables and data exports.

Realistic, partner-specific data

Because each partner’s advertisers see different inventory, metrics, and performance ranges, I built a synthetic dataset modeled on this partner’s environment. The data stayed consistent across every view, so filters, totals, and metric definitions behaved as they would in a live account, giving the partner a credible preview of what their advertisers would actually see.

Impact

01Rapid prototyping

From concept to functional product demo.

02Data privacy

Realistic, partner-specific data without exposing any real advertiser information.

03Hands-on evaluation

Let the partner test real workflows instead of reviewing static screens.
