# Building a better way to manage technical integrations

*Managing interconnected product initiatives, technical dependencies, and cross-functional launch readiness.*

- **Tag:** Program management
- **Company:** Integral Ad Science
- **Role:** Solutions Engineer
- **Deliverable:** Integration management framework

## At a glance
- Challenge: Technical partner integrations lacked a consistent project-management process, making ownership, dependencies, and launch readiness difficult to manage across teams.
- What I did: Created an integrated roadmap and tracker, formalized launch-readiness criteria, and established a weekly review of risks and dependencies across connected workstreams.
- Outcome: Clearer ownership and earlier risk detection, and adoption of the framework across Solutions Engineering organization.

## Context

Technical integrations often involved several connected workstreams, but there was no consistent process for managing them. During one partner initiative, separate plans and milestones made it difficult to track overall progress, ownership, and dependencies.

## Approach

I designed a full integration lifecycle process for coordinating three interconnected workstreams as one program. My goal was to both improve delivery of the current integration and give future integrations a more efficient, repeatable foundation.

## Solution

### Integrated program plan

I brought the three connected workstreams into a single roadmap, making phases, milestones, and dependencies visible across the program so potential risks and blockers could be identified and addressed early. I also created a centralized tracker that broke each workstream into actionable deliverables with clear owners and status, giving stakeholders a shared view of progress and accountability.

> **Visual: Integration roadmap**
>
> A six-month plan bringing three workstreams into one roadmap, with launch milestones and the dependencies between workstreams.
> - 01 Platform Migration: Discovery & requirements · Technical specification · Environment setup · Development testing · Production testing · Migration & cutover · General availability
> - 02 Measurement Expansion: Discovery & requirements · Technical specification · Development testing · Production testing · Launch readiness · General availability
> - 03 New Product Integration: Discovery & requirements · Technical specification · Initial product validation · Development testing · Production testing · Advertiser beta · Launch readiness · General availability

### Standardized testing and launch readiness

I formalized launch readiness criteria into clear checkpoints for each phase, with defined owners and sign-offs, giving all stakeholders shared visibility into when a workstream was ready to advance.

> **Visual: Launch readiness framework**
>
> Three stages, each ending in a gate with clear criteria and sign-offs.
> - Stage 1 · Define (Discovery, Technical specification) → Gate 1 · Ready to build → Validate
>   - Scope, use cases and success criteria signed off
>   - Technical spec and data requirements documented
>   - Dependencies and owners identified
>   - Sign-off: Partner, Solutions Engineering
> - Stage 2 · Validate (Development testing, Production testing) → Gate 2 · Ready for beta → Launch
>   - Test cases passed with no critical defects open
>   - Production data matches expected results within agreed tolerance
>   - Reporting validated end to end
>   - Sign-off: Engineering, Solutions Engineering
> - Stage 3 · Launch (Beta, General availability) → Gate 3 · Go / no-go → General availability
>   - Beta acceptance criteria met and confirmed by the partner
>   - Documentation and support teams ready
>   - Monitoring and rollback plan in place
>   - Sign-off: Partner, Product, Solutions Engineering

### Proactive risk management

I introduced a RAID log to track risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies, and established a weekly internal meeting to review blockers, resolve open questions, and align on next steps.

## Impact

1. **Workstream coordination:** Improved accountability and coordination across teams.
2. **Proactive risk management:** Earlier identification of blockers and better alignment on deadlines.
3. **Cross-team adoption:** A reusable framework adopted across Solutions Engineering.
