# Winning a head-to-head evaluation against an incumbent

*Leading a head-to-head evaluation from testing methodology and customer enablement to optimization and commercial storytelling.*

- **Tag:** Competitive strategy
- **Company:** Integral Ad Science
- **Role:** Solutions Engineer
- **Deliverable:** Test design & performance optimization

## At a glance
- Challenge: A high-stakes head-to-head evaluation to displace an incumbent competitor.
- What I did: Designed the testing methodology, tracked performance daily in a central tracker and presented mid-campaign recommendations.
- Outcome: Demonstrated product and partnership value, resulting in a competitive win.

## Context

Given their revenue potential, head-to-head product evaluations receive heightened scrutiny from both customers and internal leadership. Success requires more than strong product performance. It also requires a well-designed testing methodology, ongoing performance monitoring, and a strong customer partnership.

## Approach

Using the advertiser’s success criteria, I designed the testing methodology and recommended the products, configurations, and implementation approach best suited to achieving their objectives.

## Solution

### Centralized performance tracking

I created a centralized campaign performance tracker to provide Sales, Solutions Engineering, and senior leadership with visibility into the evaluation. Updated daily, the tracker consolidated performance metrics, benchmark comparisons, key findings, and configuration changes, making it easier for stakeholders to understand campaign progress without having to pull individual reports.

### Performance analysis and optimization

I translated campaign data into actionable recommendations, identifying the drivers of performance fluctuations and opportunities for improvement. I developed and presented mid-campaign findings to the customer, explaining performance trends, recommending configuration changes, and demonstrating how our products could better support their objectives.

> **Visual: Root cause analysis**
>
> 1. The issue — Behind target: Halfway through the test, one of the client's success metrics was below its goal.
> 2. Root cause — One filter: Most of the shortfall came from a single content filter that was set too strictly.
> 3. The insight — Blocking the right audience: The filter was screening out the exact content the client wanted to reach.
> 4. The fix — On track to target: Two adjustments to the filter settings were projected to bring results back within the goal.

## Impact

1. **New business win:** Demonstrated product and partnership value against an incumbent competitor leading to a win.
2. **Customer trust:** Strengthened the relationship by acting as a hands-on partner throughout the evaluation.
3. **Leadership visibility:** Centralized performance insights for leadership to inform commercial decisions.
