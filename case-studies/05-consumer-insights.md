# Turning consumer insights into actionable marketing strategies

*Using consumer research to help brands identify audience opportunities and inform marketing strategy.*

- **Tag:** Consumer insights
- **Company:** Nexxen
- **Role:** Analyst, Advanced Insights & Data
- **Deliverable:** Client-facing research presentations

## At a glance
- Challenge: Sales needed research-backed collateral to open prospect conversations, while clients needed audience insights to inform media strategy.
- What I did: Analyzed consumer interests, behaviors, demographics, and trends, then translated the findings into client presentations to inform media buying strategies.
- Outcome: Research that helped Sales open conversations and helped brand and agency teams plan campaigns.

## Context

At Nexxen, my research supported both new business conversations and campaign planning. Sales used tailored research presentations to start conversations with prospects about their customers and market. For brand and agency teams planning a media buy, the research provided a basis for deciding which audiences to prioritize and why.

## Approach

I worked with Sales to understand what each client wanted to learn, then used a proprietary consumer intelligence platform and indexed scores to compare segments, find what made audiences distinct, and spot timely trends.

## Solution

### Audience research and media recommendations

I translated research findings into client-facing presentations that combined data visualizations, consumer insights, and actionable recommendations. These included audience profiles, competitive comparisons, and trend analysis, tailored to the client’s business objectives.

> **Visual: Audience strategy brief**
>
> *Who is the next electric vehicle buyer?* Business question: Which audiences should an automotive brand target for an electric vehicle campaign? Values are indexes, where 100 is the baseline.
> - Audience: Interests of prospective EV buyers. Clean energy 185, Consumer technology 162, Hybrid vehicles 148, Outdoor recreation 135, Personal finance 124. Interpretation: Prioritize technology enthusiasts and sustainability-minded consumers; test hybrid vehicle shoppers as an adjacent audience.
> - Competitors: EV purchase interest by audience segment. Technology enthusiasts 148, Hybrid vehicle shoppers 125, General auto shoppers 112. Interpretation: Technology enthusiasts show stronger relative interest, while hybrid shoppers offer an additional audience to test.
> - Trends: EV research interest over time. Jan 100, Feb 112, Mar 108, Apr 135, May 148, Jun 162. Interpretation: In this example, rising spring interest suggests testing campaigns around Earth Day and the spring auto-shopping season.
> - Strategy: Recommended audience strategy. 1) Core audience: Technology enthusiasts and sustainability-minded consumers. 2) Expansion audience: Hybrid vehicle shoppers; emphasize range and charging convenience. 3) Campaign timing: Test messaging around Earth Day and spring auto-shopping season. 4) Measurement: Evaluate response and refine targeting.

## Impact

1. **Sales enablement:** Strategic materials for opening conversations with prospective clients.
2. **Audience strategy:** Clear recommendations on who to target and why.
3. **Market intelligence:** Timely insights into emerging trends and major events.
